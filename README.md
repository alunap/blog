# Data Science/Data Engineering musings

This is an occasional blog about things that strike me as interesting in either data science, data engineering, or the analysis of ecological, or behavioural data.

## Languages

(computer languages, that is) that I am using at this time are R, Julia, Python, and SQL, although I have a very long history with Smalltalk, and a shorter one with Ruby, Objective-C, and Swift.

## Technology

Much of what I do day-to-day is around model building in Scikit-Learn and PyTorch/Huggingface, or data pipelines in Databricks and dbt. I also engage in research in behavioural ecology, so JAGS, NIMBLE, and Turing will get a look in.
